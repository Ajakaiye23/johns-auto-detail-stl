06 / QUESTIONS

Good to know.

How long does a detail take?
Most full details need the car for the better part of a day, which is why booking ahead saves a wasted trip. Call (314) 882-0101 to arrange a slot.
What are your hours?
Monday to Friday 8:00am to 5:00pm and Saturday 8:00am to 2:00pm. Closed Sundays.
Can you fix cloudy headlights?
Yes. Yellowed, hazy lenses are sanded back and re-polished — considerably cheaper than new housings, and you can see properly at night again.
Will buffing damage my paint?
No. Machine buffing cuts back the dull oxidised top layer so the colour underneath returns, then it is sealed with wax so the finish lasts rather than just looking good for a week.
Do you clean the inside as well?
Yes — seats, carpets, vents, wheels, engine bay and trunk. The whole car, not just the parts people look at first.
